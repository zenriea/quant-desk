Research

Research terms
Sealed test
Historical data held back until this experiment’s rules and settings are fixed. Earlier experiments may have tested overlapping dates.
Gates
Checks an idea must pass before it qualifies for a regular paper allocation.
Probe
A small forward paper test. Starting one does not mean an idea passed its gates or has a proven edge.
Sharpe and PSR
Sharpe compares return with variability. Probabilistic Sharpe (PSR) estimates how likely Sharpe is to exceed a reference level; deflated PSR also discounts the effect of trying multiple ideas.
Causal DSL
A restricted language for trading rules that can only use information available at the time of a decision.
Similar settings
Checks whether small changes to an idea’s settings still yield positive results.
